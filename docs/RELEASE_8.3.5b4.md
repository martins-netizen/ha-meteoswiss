## Measured HGT history that continues

8.3.5b4 adds official SwissMetNet OGD **HGT 12/20 observations** for the selected
weather-measurement station. It backfills available daily, monthly and annual
products and continues checking new observations and corrections. These are
station-temperature indices, not measured building energy use. Existing HGT
forecasts keep their current identity and forecast meaning.

A translated **Heating degree days 12/20 last observed day** sensor appears on
the existing measurement-station device. In German, look for **Heizgradtage
12/20 letzter Messtag**. The entity ID is generated by HA; use the name and
station device rather than guessing a suffix. It becomes available only after
native Recorder import/verification, with `statistics_status: ready`, the
completed UTC `source_day`, available `statistic_ids` and source coverage counts.
A missing value is not zero; delayed or failed data is shown unavailable.

The initial background refresh imports official source history without delaying
normal weather startup. Current products are rechecked every six hours; daily
history at least weekly and at a UTC year change. All period timestamps retain
their official UTC start meaning, including DST boundaries. Monthly/annual
history comes from the separate official products and can begin earlier than
daily history. Revisions update the affected amount and cumulative known sums;
omitted old dates block an incomplete source rather than authorising deletion.

Use a native **Statistics graph** with `change` and the corresponding daily,
monthly or annual external ID. The live sensor lists available IDs, for example
`meteoswiss:klo_hgt_12_20_daily`. The one-value Statistic card has different
sparse-row aggregation and is not the default calendar-total recommendation.
See [sources, chart examples, missing data and corrections](HGT_OGD_PLAN.md).

The feature needs Recorder and a MeteoSwiss measurement-station entry. It is
not fabricated for an Open-Meteo current-weather entry. Disabling only the live
sensor hides it while the independent histories continue. Disabling the entry
stops its polling and retains its explicitly owned history for re-enablement.
Entries selecting the same station share its products instead of duplicating
or mixing data.

## Clear ownership and complete removal

Only station ownership is persisted outside Recorder; CSVs, ETags and snapshots
are memory-only, with no replacement archive. A station change exposes **Remove
unused official HGT statistics** in Repairs. Opening/cancelling deletes nothing;
confirming permanently removes the exact retired station series and metadata
after native completion/readback. A configured peer, including a disabled peer,
protects shared history. Unknown ownership or changed metadata blocks mutation.

**Removing the final MeteoSwiss integration entry also permanently removes its
owned OGD HGT statistics, including retired station products.** Ordinary sensor
history, forecasts, wind, precipitation and unrelated series remain. Complete
retired-station repairs while the integration is loaded, and remove entries
before uninstalling code. Cleanup failures retain ownership evidence and a
visible non-fixable notice. If preserving deleted projections matters, retain a
usable protected full HA backup that includes both Recorder and ownership
storage. A software downgrade cannot undo database imports or deletions.
See [ownership, removal and recovery limits](HGT_OGD_PLAN.md#ownership-and-complete-removal).

The accepted **8.3.5b3 obsolete-forecast cleanup remains complete**. It is a
different repair, not a prerequisite to be repeated and not input to measured
OGD history. Do not create/delete old data, switch stations or remove the
integration just to test this package. Follow the [short measured-HGT check](ACCEPTANCE_8.3.5b4.md).
