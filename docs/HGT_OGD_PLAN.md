## What users get

For a configured MeteoSwiss measurement station with Recorder enabled, a new
translated **Heating degree days 12/20 last observed day** sensor shows the last
completed official UTC day's value. Find it on the existing weather-station
device. Its entity ID is generated by HA; search by its translated name or
`hgt_measured` translation key, not a hard-coded entity ID. It is distinct from
today's forecast HGT and remains unavailable until import verification succeeds.
HGT is a climatic index derived from station temperatures, not a measurement of
your building's energy consumption.

The first optional background refresh backfills all available official HGT
products for that station; it does not delay ordinary current-weather setup.
Thereafter the integration rechecks current products every six hours and daily
history at least weekly, including the full documented five-day quality window.
History is revalidated at a UTC year change too. Source/Recorder outages leave
existing verified history intact and make the live sensor unavailable. A last
observed day older than two UTC days is marked delayed/unavailable rather than
presented as current. A genuine zero is still a valid observation.

This feature requires a MeteoSwiss station, Recorder and the existing HA
2026.11.0 minimum. An Open-Meteo current-weather entry does not invent measured
HGT. Disabling just the live entity hides that entity; independent external
statistics continue while the integration entry is enabled. Disabling the
entry stops its polling but keeps its owned station history for re-enablement.

## Official products and time meaning

MeteoSwiss directly provides these distinct SwissMetNet OGD products:

| Product | Parameter | External statistic ID for example station KLO |
|---|---|---|
| Daily HGT 12/20 | `xno000d0` | `meteoswiss:klo_hgt_12_20_daily` |
| Monthly total | `xno000m0` | `meteoswiss:klo_hgt_12_20_monthly` |
| Annual total | `xno000y0` | `meteoswiss:klo_hgt_12_20_annual` |

`xno012d0` is a different ATD12 measure and is never substituted. Homogenised
NBCN monthly/annual `xhs000m0/y0` are different products and are not mixed in.
The source station is the selected **measurement station**, independently of
the weather forecast point, pollen station and warning postcode.

The [official parameter catalogue](https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.meteoschweiz.ogd-smn/ogd-smn_meta_parameters.csv)
and station STAC item select exact canonical daily historical/recent and
monthly/yearly CSVs. The [station documentation](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/de/a-data-groundbased/a1-automatic-weather-stations)
recommends supplied aggregates. The [download conventions](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/general/download)
define semicolon-separated CP1252 CSVs with UTC reference times at the **start**
of each day, month or year. No previous-day shift or Swiss DST/local-midnight
conversion is applied. An annual point dated 1 January represents that completed
year, not an instantaneous reading or a value for the following year.

Only fully completed periods enter Recorder. Blank fields stay missing; no
interpolation or zero-filling occurs. A supplied zero remains zero. Source
precision is one decimal. The catalogue labels HGT as `°C`; the integration
keeps its degree-day display convention `°C·d` with unchanged numeric amounts,
not a temperature-offset conversion. Official monthly/annual amounts are
already period totals: do not multiply them by period length.

Available history differs by product and station. As a **15 November 2026 KLO
source sample**, non-missing daily values begin 1 January 1981, monthly values
in March 1950 and annual values in 1951. These are source observations, not a
promise for every station or a permanent history boundary. Earlier blank daily
rows do not justify fabricated daily values for the longer aggregate histories.
The [FAQ](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/general/faq) describes rolling
quality control; recent numbers may be revised and are not independently
certified by this integration.

## Charts, gaps and corrections

Each external row stores the official product amount as `state`. Its `sum` is
the cumulative total of **known amounts in that same product**; it is not an
observed lifetime total when source values are missing. Native `change` gives
the period amount and includes the first real period without a synthetic
zero-baseline row. Daily, monthly and annual totals are never added together.

Use a **Statistics graph** card: the **daily ID with change** for observed
daily HGT, the **monthly ID with change** for official monthly totals and the
**annual ID with change** for official annual totals. These instructions refer
to the native graph query, not the different sparse-row aggregation of the
single-value Statistic card. Do not use that card as an authoritative calendar
total without separately validating its period/baseline behaviour. The sensor's `statistic_ids` attribute lists available
IDs, alongside `source_day`, UTC reference, imported point counts and explicit
blank-field counts after the first observation. Those blank counts are not an
independent audit of omitted calendar days. Do not select `mean` or mistake the
cumulative `sum` for the one-period amount. A chart's displayed local date label
can differ from the original UTC boundary; the source meaning remains UTC.
Use supplied monthly/yearly products for aggregate comparisons, especially
where daily history is incomplete. Do not calculate old measured HGT from
forecast archives, nor relabel forecast statistics as observations.

Example for KLO, using the [native Statistics graph card](https://www.home-assistant.io/dashboards/statistics-graph/):

```yaml
type: statistics-graph
title: Observed HGT 12/20 per day
entities:
  - meteoswiss:klo_hgt_12_20_daily
stat_types:
  - change
period: day
chart_type: bar
days_to_show: 31
```

For official monthly/annual bars, choose the corresponding ID and `period:
month` / `period: year`; change `days_to_show` to cover the intended history.
Replace KLO with the station code listed by the live sensor.

Later source corrections update the affected official amount and its subsequent
cumulative sums. Explicit blank withdrawals remove only that product's exact
native hour slot. An omitted previously imported date, malformed/failed asset,
foreign station or ownership/metadata conflict blocks the update instead of
erasing unseen history. All three products are preflighted before mutations;
native APIs commit batches and a complete readback is required before `ready`.
An unconditional native `SynchronizeTask` is queued behind imports: a merely
empty queue does not prove that its last running task has committed. A controlled
native regression holds that final writer before its transaction to verify this
boundary.
Recorder has no atomic multi-product transaction: interrupted partial commits
remain owned and are reconciled on the next successful refresh, never falsely
reported as verified. Revisions change past source values; this is not a
versioned archive of every original publication.

## Ownership and complete removal

**Unreleased pending-import correction:** a native queued-write barrier and
retired-station serialization now precede ownership pruning. This closes a
reproduced station-change/cancellation race during the first import; already
queued writes can outlive their caller. Timeouts retain provenance instead of
assuming an empty read proves absence. See [trigger, tests and recovery limits](HGT_PENDING_IMPORT_OWNERSHIP.md).
Published b4/b5 do not contain this correction; it adds no new deletion consent
or production cleanup action.

The integration persists only a set of owned station codes in its HA storage.
Observations live once in Recorder, not in a second on-disk CSV/archive. Source
representations, ETags and six-hour snapshots are memory-only and disappear
when the final active entry for that station unloads. Entries selecting the same
station share its three external histories; station IDs prevent mixed stations.
Existing compatible metadata without the matching ownership record is not
silently adopted. Keep Recorder and the integration's ownership storage in the
same protected full HA backup; restoring only one side can require review.

**After changing the weather station:** open **Settings → System → Repairs →
Remove unused official HGT statistics**. Review the exact old station IDs and
confirm complete removal if they are no longer wanted. Opening/cancelling does
nothing. Confirmation permanently deletes their long-/short-term statistic
rows and metadata, then removes their ownership references after verified
absence. It creates no archive and does not purge ordinary sensor history,
forecasts, precipitation, wind or unrelated series. A still-configured peer,
including a disabled peer, protects shared history. Resolve these repairs while
the integration is loaded, before disabling all entries or removing code.

**When removing the integration completely:** the final native HA integration
entry deletion also permanently removes all of this integration's owned OGD HGT
products, including retired stations. This is part of that explicit removal
action. Ordinary sensor history remains governed by Recorder retention. Keep a
full backup first if those official historical projections need preservation.
No generic repair confirmation after a restart with zero entries is relied on:
a custom repair cannot run without the component loaded. Remove integration
entries first and confirm successful cleanup before uninstalling integration
code from Marketplace. Removing code/files first bypasses lifecycle cleanup.

Cleanup failures and ownership conflicts leave a persistent, non-fixable notice
and retain ownership evidence; they never claim that deletion succeeded. Re-enable
or reinstall/load the integration and resolve the specific ownership/metadata
conflict, using a matching full backup where necessary. With no entries and no
loaded component, use HA's native Statistics tools to inspect the exact records;
do not guess ownership from a name prefix or purge the whole database. A
completely lost ownership record cannot safely be reconstructed from IDs alone.
A software rollback does not undo already committed external imports/deletions;
restore the matching full HA backup when data recovery is intended.

Old **forecast** statistics use the distinct accepted b3 repair described in
[the upgrade guide](UPGRADING_8.3.5.md). They are not needed for this importer and
deleting them does not interrupt the new measured series.

## Engineering and release boundary

The runtime borrows HA's verified-TLS session; it follows no redirects and uses
no new credentials, coordinates, telemetry or dependencies. Exact asset identity,
ETag/cache URL binding, wire/decoded-size and row budgets, bounded retries and
cancellation apply. Parsing runs off-loop. Native Recorder metadata/row checks,
per-station locks, durable ownership before writes, completion/readback barriers
and consent snapshots protect updates and deletion. No direct database writer,
private endpoint or unbounded arbitrary file reader is introduced.

Native tests cover both supported HAs, UTC/leap/year boundaries, source gaps,
first-period change, corrections/withdrawals, shared entries, memory ownership,
metadata/storage failure, cancelled confirmation, station changes and final
native entry removal. All four new modules retain 100% line and branch coverage.
[The quality matrix](QUALITY_SCALE.md) and [bounded privacy/security assessment](PRIVACY_SECURITY_REVIEW.md)
retain full Gold/independent-review limits. Published b4/b5 and the operator's
focused b5 observed-day availability check are complete. The pending-import
correction's [merged-source evidence](HGT_PENDING_IMPORT_OWNERSHIP.md) remains
distinct from the b6 package checks, publication and installed check.
B5 availability and published b3/PR #114 acceptance are not repeated;
full historical-row or independent assurance is not inferred from those results.
